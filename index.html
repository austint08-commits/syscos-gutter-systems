Why Install Gutter Screens?

Clogged gutters aren't just a nuisance — they're expensive. Here's what you're actually protecting against.

Eliminate Ladder Risk

Falling off a ladder is one of the most common home injuries. Every trip to the roof to clear gutters is a risk. Once we install your screens, you never climb that ladder again — cleaning is our job, not yours.

Reduce Wildfire Risk

Dry leaves and pine needles sitting in your gutters are kindling. In Sacramento's oak and pine neighborhoods, embers can ignite that debris and spread to the roof. Screens keep the fuel out.

Protect Roof & Foundation

Overflowing gutters rot fascia boards, soak siding, and dump water at your foundation. Over time that means cracking, shifting, and costly repairs. Screens keep water flowing where it should.

Save on Cleaning Costs

Professional gutter cleaning runs $150–$300 per visit, two to three times a year. Screens pay for themselves within a few seasons — and keep saving you money every year after.

Keep Pests Out

Standing water and debris in gutters attract mosquitoes, birds, and rodents. Screens cut off nesting spots and breeding grounds — protecting your roofline and your family.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from Sacramento homeowners.

Do you install on metal and tile roofs?

Yes — metal and tile roofs are our specialty. Standing seam metal and tile roofs need specific clips and mounting methods, not universal hardware. We install screens made for your exact roof type, so nothing shifts, scratches, or voids a warranty. We also install on composition shingle and flat roofs.

How much do gutter screens cost in Sacramento?

Most homes fall between $800 and $3,000 total, depending on the screen type and how many linear feet of gutter you have. Basic screen-and-clip runs about $5.50 per linear foot, and premium micro-mesh about $18. Your on-site quote gives you an exact number.

Will I get the price before work starts?

Yes, always. We give you a written quote on-site before any work begins. If something changes during the job, we stop and talk to you first.

How long do gutter screens last?

Aluminum and stainless micro-mesh screens typically last 15–25 years. Budget screen-and-clip options last around 5–10 years. We use materials rated for Sacramento's heat and sun.

Will I still need to clean my gutters?

You'll never have to scoop leaves out again. You may occasionally need to rinse the top of the screens with a hose — a 15-minute job from the ground, not a ladder-and-bucket afternoon.

Can the installation damage my roof or shingles?

No. Screens attach to the gutter itself using clips, screws, or hidden hangers. Your roof and its warranty stay untouched. On metal and tile roofs we use clips designed specifically for those surfaces.

How long does the installation take?

Most single-family homes are done in a single day. Larger homes or complex rooflines might take two. We clean up every scrap before we leave.

Do you offer gutter cleaning too?

Yes. We clean gutters as a standalone service, and it's included at no extra charge when we install new screens. If you're not ready for screens yet, we can keep your existing gutters clear — scooping, flushing, and checking downspouts.
